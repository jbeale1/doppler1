This project logs data from a doppler radar traffic speed sensor (HLK-LD2415H) and separately presents views of the statistics across multiple days.

A speed histogram based on 161 days (91k traffic events) shows well over 90% of traffic fits a normal distribution. Leaving off the slower side (neighbors, deliveries) there is about 2% of faster traffic that is outside the best-fit gaussian, forming a excess at the high end not predicted by the rest of the data.
